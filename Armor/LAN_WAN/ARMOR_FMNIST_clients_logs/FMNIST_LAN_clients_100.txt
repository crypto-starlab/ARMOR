Original notes:
Seconds; decimal MB; sender frames counted once; setup/shutdown excluded.
Dec total sums Server1 decrypt+decode and Server2 partial-decrypt compute; RPC, preparation and serialization excluded.
Dec scopes include small Python loop/result-collection overhead and native stream completion.
TX is serialization + blocking send time, not isolated network latency.
serialize_s measures pickle.dumps; blocking_send_s measures send_bytes only.
ciphertext_serialize_s separately measures ciphertext.to_bytes, outside TX; do not add it to HE or Dec.
Missing split timings in historical logs are null/blank, not inferred from TX.
delivery_s pairs message IDs: send_bytes start to recv_bytes completion, before unpickling; same-host clocks only.
First round has aggregation only; norm/similarity filtering begins in round 2.
Component sum is an accounting sum; round_wall_s is elapsed Server1 round time.
HE comp uses existing norm/similarity/aggregation timers; aggregate transform preparation is outside Dec and these HE timers.
